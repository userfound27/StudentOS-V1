StudentOS — Student Productivity & Study Planner

StudentOS is a student workspace for planning study sessions, organizing upcoming exams, tracking assessment scores, using a focus timer, and keeping long-term academic goals visible.

What StudentOS does

  • Plan and manage study sessions and tasks.
  • Track exam lessons and preparation progress.
  • Record scores and monitor academic performance.
  • Use focused study sessions with a customizable timer.
  • Keep a personal academic journey and goals visible.
